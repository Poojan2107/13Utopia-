## 1. Page Narrative & Visual Sequence

1. **Header & Navigation**:
   - Left: Lusion wordmark SVG
   - Right: Sound wave frequency toggle, "Let's talk •" pill, and "Menu ••" modal toggle.
2. **Hero & 3D Celestial Void**:
   - Dynamic Three.js WebGL particle sphere/nebula cloud with ambient glow and physics inertia.
   - Massive bottom typography: **LUSION**.
   - Subtitle: `SCROLL TO EXPLORE`.
   - Horizontal crosshairs `+` grid.
3. **Lunar Astronaut Scene & Manifesto**:
   - Camera plunges into a craggy moonscape crater terrain.
   - Solitary astronaut illuminated by a vertical celestial light beam.
   - Text reveal:
     - Left: `WE ARE LUSION A CREATIVE PRODUCTION STUDIO`
     - Right: `CRAFTING UNIQUE DIGITAL EXPERIENCES`
   - Followed by philosophy statement:
     - `_A worldwide team_ of specialists in design, motion, 3D, and technology`
     - `working together to turn ambitious ideas into immersive digital experiences.`
4. **Holographic 3D Team Showcase**:
   - Oversized letter-spaced header: `T E A M`.
   - Interactive member carousel with team index `[[ 00X ]]`, member status, role badge, and active progress bar.
   - 3D point cloud / holographic scan with digital matrix rain glyphs and animated topographical contour lines.
   - Royal blue cursor/button follower `→` for transitioning team members.
   - Studio manifesto paragraph.
5. **Studio Area of Expertise (Royal Cobalt Section)**:
   - Deep royal blue backdrop (`#0d1ba8` / `#0022f5`).
   - Massive typography: `STUDIO AREA OF EXPERTISE`.
   - 4 Interactive Playing Cards for Disciplines:
     - **Strategy** (Digital Experience Strategy, Technology Strategy, Creative Direction, Discovery, Research)
     - **Creative** (Art Direction, UX/UI Design, Motion Design, Interactive Design, Illustration)
     - **Tech** (WebGL Development, Front End Development, Unity/Unreal, Interactive Installations, AR & VR)
     - **Production** (Procedural Modeling, 3D Asset Creation, 3D Optimization, Animation, Pipeline Dev)
6. **Collaboration CTA & Confetti Simulation**:
   - `IS YOUR BIG IDEA READY TO GO WILD?`
   - `Let's work together!`
   - Reactive geometric confetti particle simulation.
   - `↓ CONTINUE TO SCROLL ↓` pill button.
7. **Awards & Recognition (58 Honors)**:
   - Breakdown of Awwwards (29), FWA (20), CSSDA (2), Webby (4), Lovie (1), Drum (1), CommArts (1).
8. **Press Articles & International Talks**:
   - Articles from Porsche Newsroom, Wallpaper*, Opera North.
   - Talks at Digital Design Days Milan, Awwwards Conf Amsterdam, KIKK Festival Namur, Grow Paris.
9. **Studio Footer & Projects Teaser**:
   - Bristol Studio address, social channels, direct business/general email contacts.

   - Floating back-to-top button.
   - Dark teaser banner linking to `/clone/lusion` projects.

## 2. Design Tokens

- **Colors**:
  - Dark Void: `#060709` / `#0a0b0e`
  - Royal Cobalt Blue: `#0c1fa8` / `#002aff`
  - Crisp White: `#ffffff`
  - Off-white light footer: `#f3f4f8`
  - Muted Slate: `#8892b0`
  - Subtle Borders: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`
- **Typography**:
  - Headings: Grotesque Sans (`Syne`, `Inter`, system sans-serif display)
  - Monospace tags & numbers: Precision monospace (`ui-monospace`, `SFMono-Regular`, `Menlo`, monospace)
- **Motion & 3D**:
  - WebGL with Three.js (particle shaders, terrain meshes, volumetric light beam, point cloud scans)
  - CSS transforms, spring hover physics, smooth scroll anchors
