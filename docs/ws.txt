# 13 UTOPIA

## WEBSITE STRATEGY & EXPERIENCE FOUNDATION

### Rebased from first principles

### v1.0

---

# 00. THE RESET

The previous website direction explored many visual concepts:

* a 13 portal
* impossible geometry
* giant typography
* sections breaking apart
* animated grids
* orbit systems
* immersive transitions
* 13-section narrative
* extreme scroll choreography
* experimental capability interfaces

Those explorations were useful.

They are now considered:

# SCRAPPED.

None of them are sacred.

We are not redesigning the old design.

We are not trying to find a prettier version of the old homepage.

We are restarting from the strategic foundation of 13 UTOPIA.

The only things carried forward are the underlying truths:

**BE UNREAL. BE UNREASONABLE.**

**CREATE. BUILD. GROW.**

13 UTOPIA is a creative technology and growth company.

It works with ambitious businesses.

It combines strategy, creativity, technology and growth.

It wants to challenge default thinking rather than merely execute instructions.

It wants to make ambitious ideas real.

The website must express all of that.

---

# 01. THE WEBSITE IS NOT THE BRAND

This distinction comes first.

The website is an expression of 13 UTOPIA.

It is not the identity itself.

Therefore:

The website does not need to prove that it is "unreasonable" every three seconds.

It does not need an animation for every idea.

It does not need to constantly break grids.

It does not need to look experimental merely because the company is experimental.

The website's responsibility is:

> **Make the brand understandable, desirable and credible.**

The identity can create curiosity.

The website must create understanding.

The work must create belief.

The proof must create trust.

The CTA must create action.

---

# 02. THE WEBSITE'S CORE JOB

The website has five jobs.

## 01 — ORIENT

Within seconds, someone should understand:

Who is 13 UTOPIA?

What does it do?

What kind of company is it?

---

## 02 — INTRIGUE

The website should make the visitor think:

> "This feels different."

Not:

> "This website has a lot of animations."

The distinction matters.

---

## 03 — QUALIFY

A visitor should quickly understand whether 13 UTOPIA is relevant to their problem.

The site needs to help them recognize:

"I need to launch."

"I need to modernize."

"I need to build."

"I need to automate."

"I need to grow."

"I need to transform."

---

## 04 — PROVE

The website needs evidence:

work

capability

thinking

technology

results

people

process

clients

experience

---

## 05 — CONVERT

The final outcome is not:

> "Wow, cool website."

It is:

> "I want to talk to these people."

---

# 03. THE WEBSITE'S SINGLE STRATEGIC QUESTION

The whole site should ultimately answer:

# "Why 13 UTOPIA?"

Not:

"What services do they have?"

Not:

"What technologies do they use?"

Not:

"How many pages does the site have?"

The visitor should leave understanding:

> **13 UTOPIA is the company you bring in when something important needs to become possible, real and successful.**

---

# 04. THE CORE WEBSITE PROMISE

The website should communicate:

> **We help ambitious businesses move from what is to what could be next.**

And then prove how:

# CREATE.

# BUILD.

# GROW.

This becomes the backbone of the experience.

---

# 05. THE WEBSITE'S INFORMATION HIERARCHY

The site should answer information in this order:

### 01 — WHO

13 UTOPIA.

### 02 — WHAT

Create, Build, Grow.

### 03 — WHY

Move beyond the obvious.

### 04 — FOR WHAT

Launch, grow, scale, modernize, automate, transform.

### 05 — HOW

Question, imagine, define, create, build, grow.

### 06 — PROOF

Work, results, people, experience.

### 07 — NEXT

Start a project.

This hierarchy is much more important than any visual treatment.

---

# 06. THE PRIMARY AUDIENCE

The site should not be designed primarily for designers, developers, or agency people.

Those audiences matter.

But the primary audience is:

# THE BUSINESS DECISION-MAKER.

Someone who has:

an ambitious objective

a business problem

a transformation underway

a product to launch

technology to replace

a process to improve

growth to unlock

or a new direction to create.

The site must therefore work for someone who does not care what framework the developers use.

They care:

Can these people understand my problem?

Can they think?

Can they execute?

Can they make this work?

# 52. ACCESSIBILITY IS NOT OPTIONAL

The content cannot be inaccessible.

Required baseline:

reasonable contrast

usable touch targets

animations that never prevent comprehension

The previous source already established accessibility and reduced motion as part of the intended experience philosophy.

# 53. PERFORMANCE PRINCIPLE

This is critical.

# THE WEBSITE SHOULD FEEL EXPENSIVE WITHOUT FEELING HEAVY.

The experience may use:

GSAP

ScrollTrigger

SVG

3D

video

large imagery

custom interactions

But these should be layered strategically.

Not everywhere.

The earlier technical philosophy was explicit about limiting WebGL/Three.js to a small number of signature moments, using SVG/CSS heavily, respecting reduced motion, and preventing critical content from depending on JavaScript.

That remains a good implementation principle.

---

# 54. MOTION STRATEGY

We are not defining individual animations yet.

We are defining the role of motion.

Motion can do four things:

### 01 — DIRECT

Help the user understand hierarchy.

### 02 — CONNECT

Show relationships between concepts.

### 03 — REVEAL

Introduce information progressively.

### 04 — EXPRESS

Add character.

Anything beyond those categories should be questioned.

---

# 55. MOTION SHOULD NOT

Hide content.

Slow down comprehension.

Force every visitor into a choreography.

Require a high-end GPU.

Make mobile unusable.

Turn text into inaccessible canvas content.

Exist simply because:

> "We can animate this."

---

# 56. MOBILE STRATEGY

desktop minus 60%.

Mobile is its own composition.

The strategic experience remains:

understand

explore

prove

convert

But interactions should be simplified.

Do not force:

hover states

cursor mechanics

massive pinned scenes

complex 3D

horizontal scroll

into tiny screens unless they genuinely help.

# 57. MOBILE PRIORITY

# CLARITY > NOVELTY

# SPEED > SPECTACLE

The identity can still be unmistakable.

# 70. WEBSITE LENGTH

But it should be long because the story earns the length.

Not because:

> "We need 12 sections."

remove it.

# 71. SECTION DECISION FILTER

Before allowing a section into the homepage, ask:

### WHAT QUESTION DOES THIS ANSWER?

If there is no clear answer:

remove it.

### WHY DOES THIS NEED TO LIVE ON THE HOMEPAGE?

If the answer is:

"because it looks cool,"

move it or remove it.

### WHAT DOES IT PROVE?

If nothing:

reconsider it.

### WHAT SHOULD THE USER DO AFTER THIS?

Not every section needs a CTA.

But every section should have a role.
